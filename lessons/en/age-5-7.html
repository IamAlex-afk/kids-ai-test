Lesson 1 of 5

🤖 Is Alexa alive?

Alexa, Siri and Google Assistant are not alive. They are programs that can answer, like a music box: it plays music, but it can't feel it. They can't feel happy or sad. They can be helpful — but they aren't real friends.

Example

🎵 A music box plays a beautiful tune — but it can't hear the music. Alexa talks — but she doesn't understand the words.

Check yourself

Is Alexa alive like a dog or cat?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: No, she is a program 🤖

Alexa is a program! Like a clock: it shows the time, but it can't feel time passing.

Source: Druga et al., "Hey Google is it OK if I eat you?", MIT Media Lab, IDC 2017

Lesson 2 of 5

🧩 AI guesses the next word

AI has "read" a huge amount of text and noticed which words usually go together. When you ask it something, it guesses which word usually comes next, then the next one, and that's how an answer appears. It does NOT think or understand like you do.

Example

📚 If you read 1000 fairy tales, it's easy to guess: "Once upon a…" — next comes "time". AI guesses the same way — just from a huge, huge amount of text!

Check yourself

Does AI understand a story like you do?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: No, it guesses words 🤖

AI guesses which word usually comes next. Understanding a story and caring about the heroes — that's what YOU can do!

Source: LeCun, Bengio, Hinton, "Deep Learning", Nature 2015

Lesson 3 of 5

🛡️ AI is a TOOL — you are the BOSS

AI is a very helpful tool, like a calculator. A calculator can't make you love maths — and AI can't make your choices for you. YOU decide.

Example

✏️ A pencil is a tool. It can't draw by itself — YOU make the drawing. AI is the same: you are the artist.

Check yourself

Who is in charge — you or the AI?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: I AM! I'm the boss 💪

YOU are in charge! AI is your helper, not your boss.

Source: UNESCO, "AI competency framework for students", 2024

Lesson 4 of 5

🎨 AI can draw pretend pictures

Some pictures on the internet were never drawn by a person at all — a computer made them! You type words like "a cat wearing a hat" and AI creates a brand new picture. It's not magic — it learned from a huge, huge number of pictures.

Example

🖼️ It's like mixing paint colours you know to make a new colour — except AI mixes what it learned from pictures.

Check yourself

Did a real artist draw every picture you see online?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: No, some are made by AI! 🤖

AI can create brand-new pictures from words. It has never held a paintbrush — it learned from the pictures it was shown.

Source: LeCun, Bengio, Hinton, "Deep Learning", Nature 2015

Lesson 5 of 5

🔍 Why grown-ups double-check what AI says

AI can make mistakes without even knowing it! That's why a grown-up should always check important AI answers — just like checking a recipe before you bake cookies.

Example

🍪 If a recipe said "add 100 cups of sugar", it's easy to see that's wrong. Grown-ups check AI the same way — with their own thinking.

Check yourself

Should you check AI answers with a grown-up?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: Yes, always! 👍

AI can be wrong sometimes. A grown-up helper makes sure everything is safe and correct.

Source: Ji et al., "Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation", ACM Computing Surveys, 2023
