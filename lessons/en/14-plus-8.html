✍️ Getting better answers: how to prompt AI well

This device has no voice for this language. Ask an adult to read the lesson aloud.

The quality of an AI's answer depends heavily on how you ask. Being specific, giving context, specifying the format you want, and asking the AI to "think step by step" all measurably improve output quality — this isn't a trick, it's a documented effect of how these models process context.

Example

🎯 "Write about dogs" gets a generic answer. "Write a 100-word comparison of Labrador and Poodle temperaments for a first-time dog owner" gets something actually useful. Specificity is the whole game.

Check yourself

Why does asking AI to "think step by step" often improve its answers?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: It conditions the output on reasoning-like patterns from training data 📊

Step-by-step prompting steers the model toward patterns associated with careful reasoning in its training data — measurably reducing errors on complex tasks.

Source: Wei et al., "Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models", Google Research 2022
