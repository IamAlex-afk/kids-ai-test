🤖 What IS a robot?

For ages 5–7: an adult reads the lesson together with the child.

This device has no voice for this language. Ask an adult to read the lesson aloud.

A robot is a machine that follows rules. Alexa, Siri, and Google — they are not alive. They are like a very smart toy that listens and talks. But they can't feel happy or sad. She can be helpful — but she isn't a real friend.

Example

🎮 A video game follows rules too! If you press jump, Mario jumps. AI is the same — it follows rules, it doesn't think.

Check yourself

Is Alexa alive like a dog or cat?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: No, she is a machine 🤖

Alexa is a machine! She follows rules, just like a clock follows rules to show the right time.

Source: MIT Media Lab, Early Childhood AI
