📝 When AI gets your homework wrong

This device has no voice for this language. Ask an adult to read the lesson aloud.

Using AI to help with homework can be smart — but submitting AI mistakes as your own work can hurt your grade AND your learning. The safest approach: use AI to explain a concept, then write the answer yourself, in your own words.

Example

🏋️ Using AI for every answer is like having someone else lift your weights at the gym — you won't actually get stronger, even if the weight got lifted.

Check yourself

What's the smartest way to use AI for homework?

Show the answer and explanation

Correct answer: Use it to understand, then write your own answer ✍️

AI is a great study helper — but the actual thinking and writing should still be yours, so you actually learn.
