Workshop: AI activities

Short hands-on activities: guess, try, see the result, understand it and solve a new example. No sign-up; answers are not saved or sent anywhere.

  1. Why did the sorter get it wrong?Find what made the sorter wrong and fix it with examples. Compare "before" and "after".
  2. Check a confident answerFind the mistake in a confident answer, fix it with the document, and tell "false" from "cannot be decided".
  3. What is OK to share?Remove personal details from a request while keeping the task, and explain why each piece is needed or not.

All lessons by age
