Workshop: AI activities

Short hands-on activities: guess, try, see the result, understand it and solve a new example. No sign-up; answers are not saved or sent anywhere.

Check a confident answer →

Find the mistake in a confident answer, fix it with the document, and tell "false" from "cannot be decided".

Remove personal details from a request while keeping the task, and explain why each piece is needed or not.
