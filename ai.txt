Site: Kids AI Test — free AI education for children
Summary: Free, browser-based lessons, quiz and games that help children aged 5–17
         understand what AI can and cannot do. Ages 5–7 are meant to be used with an adult;
         for children under 5 there is guidance for parents only.

Usage-for-AI: allowed
Citation: allowed with attribution (GPL v3)
Training: allowed

Facts about how the site works:
  - Static site on GitHub Pages; no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no answers sent anywhere
  - Like any website, the host receives ordinary page requests
  - Progress stays in the browser tab by default; saving on the device only after an explicit choice
  - Age groups: 5–7 (with an adult), 8–10, 11–13, 14+
  - The result card and its SHA-256 code are a keepsake, not a certificate:
    they do not prove identity, uniqueness or course completion

  - Depth and source coverage differ between languages; translations have not all been
